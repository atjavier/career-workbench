# Privacy, Local-First & Shared-Direction Review

**Scope:** `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` only. This is a review; it makes no change to either spine.

## Verdict

**Needs revision before implementation.** The intended local-authoritative, consent-led model is strong, and the dark-shell/warm-action/Resume-editor direction is explicitly adopted. The remaining gaps are concentrated around untrusted-document rendering, the actual local data boundary, and tokenized visual behavior.

| Severity | Location | Finding | Required resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | EXPERIENCE.md:36-38, 94, 216, 222-224 | Imported PDFs, selected folders, source excerpts, and the read-only Resume preview have no safe-rendering contract. Nothing forbids embedded active content, remote assets, telemetry, or a renderer silently sending document content off device. The preview is therefore not demonstrably local-first. | Define a sandboxed/local rendering rule: do not execute document content or load remote assets; disclose every non-local processor before use; preserve a safe text-only/failure view; and identify the renderer and its storage boundary before release. |
| High | EXPERIENCE.md:44, 128, 164, 218 | Retention, final deletion, recovery-copy lifetime, backup, and authentication are acknowledged as open yet the product promises private, local recovery. This is a release-blocking privacy contract, not an implementation detail: the UI cannot truthfully explain deletion, sign-out, or shared-device exposure until it is decided. | Resolve these policies before build and make the Data & storage surface reflect the actual local location, encryption/account binding, retention, export, deletion, backup, and post-sign-out behavior. |
| High | EXPERIENCE.md:21, 123, 163, 191, 215 | The AI transfer preview is good, but it does not establish a default processing mode or prohibit fallback from a local model/manual path to a remote provider. “Retention setting” is also ambiguous: it could describe a provider policy rather than a user-controlled consent. | Specify local/manual as the default where available; require a new per-request consent for any remote provider, model, region, retention change, or extra attachment; prohibit silent fallback; and name what the retention setting means and who controls it. |
| High | EXPERIENCE.md:104, 128, 164, 208 | Google revocation stops future access and distinguishes remote retention, but gives no actionable remote-data lifecycle. A user can believe a local delete/disconnect removed a sensitive mirror that still exists in a Sheet, including historical rows or versions. | For every disconnect/delete flow, state exactly what remains remotely, link to the owning sheet, offer an explicit scoped remote-cleanup action where feasible, and record/announce the outcome separately from local deletion. |
| High | EXPERIENCE.md:124, 128, 163 | “Preserve locally” and “private to the authenticated user” lack a session-lock, local-at-rest, and reauthentication contract for exports, recovery, data export, and connection changes. On a shared or stolen device, sign-out protection alone is insufficient. | Define idle-lock/sign-out behavior, local encryption or equivalent protection, and reauthentication for sensitive exports, deletion, recovery restoration, and Google connection changes. Do not present private-to-user language until these guarantees exist. |
| Medium | EXPERIENCE.md:94, 102, 124, 155; DESIGN.md:107, 115 | The editor/preview direction does not define when the preview is current, what it contains while edits are unsaved, or how preview/export provenance is tied to the same revision. A stale preview beside current controls can cause the user to approve/export material they did not inspect. | Give preview, review acknowledgement, and export a shared visible revision/time state; mark stale/unsaved preview clearly; regenerate locally or require an explicit update; invalidate acknowledgement when the reviewed revision changes. |
| Medium | EXPERIENCE.md:222-223 | **Refresh Library** and **Document for Resume** say there is no passive scan/watch and a selected folder is identified, but do not define the folder permission scope, recursive-file policy, metadata collection, or how that authority is revoked. This leaves a broad local-file access boundary hidden behind a friendly Resume action. | Show the exact folder and scope before access; default to user-selected, bounded files; state whether subfolders/metadata are read; provide revoke/clear-source controls; and keep proposed evidence and source paths local unless separately disclosed. |
| Medium | DESIGN.md:15-16, 54-56, 78 | The specified warm primary button is internally inconsistent with its stated WCAG requirement: `#FFFFFF` on `#F59E4A` measures **2.13:1**, not 4.5:1. This affects the shared action treatment across every workspace. | Change the foreground or primary shade and validate all button states against their actual backgrounds; do not rely on the prose contrast intent. |
| Medium | DESIGN.md:74, 78, 103-118; EXPERIENCE.md:25, 83-105 | “Dark premium shell” and “warm next explicit commitment” are direction statements, not a complete shared component contract. Header navigation/active/focus/compact behavior and action hierarchy for destructive actions (delete, revoke, discard) are unspecified; a warm primary confirmation could visually normalize an irreversible action. | Define the header’s navigation, active, hover/focus, mobile, and status behavior plus a separate destructive-action treatment. Restrict warm primary to affirmative, reversible/forward commitments and reserve danger styling with clear effect copy for destructive commitments. |
| Low | EXPERIENCE.md:19, 36-37, 222; DESIGN.md:70, 74 | The navigation names **Resume** and **Evidence Library** as distinct destinations, while the IA and approved change repeatedly call the surface “Resume & Evidence Library.” This weakens the new Resume-editor/preview direction and risks an inconsistent header hierarchy. | Name the destination model explicitly: separate tabs with a defined handoff, or a single combined surface. Ensure the dark-header labels, Resume stepper, and editor/preview entry point use the same terminology. |
| Low | EXPERIENCE.md:21, 104, 163 | The connection/status copy has strong disclosure requirements, but there is no persistent, glanceable indicator of which remote connection is currently enabled on a privacy-sensitive workspace. Users may not recognize that a later Generate or tracker update can cross a boundary. | In the shared shell/status region, show a text-first local/remote connection state with a direct route to inspect or disable it; it must not imply an active transfer when none is occurring. |

## Positive controls to preserve

- Manual-only refresh, no automatic retry, and local-authoritative tracker recovery.
- Per-request AI transfer preview, separate training/improvement opt-in, and notes-local-only default for Sheets.
- The explicit dark-header, warm-action, and labelled editor-plus-read-only-preview direction—once its component and revision contracts are completed.
