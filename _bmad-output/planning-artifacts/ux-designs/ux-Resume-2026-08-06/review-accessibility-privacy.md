# Accessibility, Privacy & Safe-Handoff Review

**Verdict: needs revision before finalization.** The spines establish a strong evidence-led, non-autonomous workflow and cover several baseline safeguards (manual-only refresh, non-destructive sync failures, visible focus, labelled state, and pre-integration disclosures). However, important WCAG 2.2 interaction requirements and the product's data-lifecycle/consent contract remain unspecified. The findings below are UX-contract gaps, not implementation defects.

| Severity | Location | Finding | Required UX fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | EXPERIENCE.md: Foundation; AI and privacy settings; Open Question 6 | There is no data-lifecycle surface or flow for exporting, deleting, retaining, or clearing Candidate Profile, local recovery copies, source records, material versions, audit data, and Google connection metadata. “Preserve locally” is required, but its location, duration, identity binding, and disposal are unknown. This conflicts with minimization and makes shared-device/recovery risk unreviewable. | Add **Data & storage** settings and a named flow: show each data class, storage location/retention, export/delete effects, recovery-copy handling, and a confirmation/re-auth step for destructive actions. Define what remains after Google revocation and what is removed; make local recovery data private to the authenticated account and inaccessible after sign-out. Resolve retention/deletion before build. |
| High | EXPERIENCE.md: Foundation; Component Patterns / Draft workspace | First-use AI disclosure is only a disclosure. It does not state an affirmative, recorded choice for provider training/improvement, nor a per-action data preview/minimization boundary. The PRD forbids such use unless explicitly opted in. | Before the first transmission, show the exact fields/attachments and job text to be sent, provider/model and retention setting, cost estimate, and separate optional training/improvement control defaulting off. Persist an auditable consent state; allow change before generation and make “draft locally” equally available. Never bundle this consent with generation or Google authorization. |
| High | EXPERIENCE.md: Google Sheets connection and sync status; UJ-4 | OAuth flow lacks a clear account identity, exact spreadsheet/worksheet and data-column preview, scope-change handling, and post-authorization confirmation. A user can unknowingly mirror sensitive notes/interview data to the wrong Google account/sheet. | Require a review-before-connect step that names Google account, requested least-privilege scope, spreadsheet + worksheet, categories/fields to mirror (including notes), and whether creation/update is enabled. Reconfirm on account, sheet, scope, or schema change; default sensitive notes to local-only until explicitly included. Show a non-secret connection identifier and a disconnect/revoke outcome distinguishing local deletion from remote data retention. |
| Medium | EXPERIENCE.md: Accessibility Floor; State Patterns | The accessibility floor says states are announced but does not define live-region behavior, priority, deduplication, or completion focus. Refresh and sync progress could create noisy announcements or leave keyboard/screen-reader users unaware of partial completion and recovery actions. | Specify a persistent programmatic status region: announce start and terminal/partial outcomes once, expose per-source/entity rows on demand, use assertive announcements only for blocking errors, and move focus only on an explicitly requested results/recovery view. Include an accessible summary with links to affected rows. |
| Medium | EXPERIENCE.md: Accessibility Floor; Component Patterns / Confirmation dialog; DESIGN.md: Components | Keyboard coverage omits WCAG 2.2 focus appearance/not-obscured requirements, minimum target size, and a non-pointer alternative for any reorderable Interview Rounds or dense row actions. “Visible focus” alone is insufficient. | Require a 24-by-24 CSS-pixel minimum target or documented spacing exception; a focus indicator with sufficient area/contrast that remains visible when sticky navigation, dialogs, or toasts appear; and keyboard-accessible Move up/Move down controls (or equivalent) for ordered rounds. Document a logical table/card reading order and persistent column/action labels at zoom. |

Blocking issues may be visually reached but not demonstrably exposed through the reading order. | Provide a review summary with total claims, unresolved warnings, and provenance links; define completion as an explicit user acknowledgement after the accessible summary, not viewport exposure. Ensure each warning has a unique text name, programmatic association with its claim, and keyboard shortcut/link to the repair control; re-require acknowledgement after material edits. |
| Medium | EXPERIENCE.md: UJ-2/UJ-4; outbound handoff primitive | The original-application handoff names the external boundary but does not make the destination host or personal-data boundary explicit. Disabled export controls and muted metadata/status states are likely to be relied on in a data-dense UI but lack a text/contrast/focus rule. | Define text, status, disabled, and focus-indicator contrast thresholds (including non-text UI components), and ensure disabled status is not the sole communication of why export is unavailable; retain readable explanatory text and a reachable remediation link. |

## Positive controls to retain

- Manual, bounded refreshes and source-specific recovery avoid opaque collection behavior.
- Evidence/provenance, non-predictive Fit Labels, and export blocking provide a sound truthful-materials foundation.
- Local-authoritative tracker behavior and entity-level sync errors reduce loss and accidental duplicate writes.

## Exit criteria

Before final status, resolve the three High findings and add the Medium requirements to the spine. Validate keyboard-only and screen-reader journeys for UJ-1 through UJ-4 at 200% reflow/zoom, including partial refresh, unsupported-claim repair, OAuth denial/revocation, wrong-sheet prevention, and a Sheets conflict.
