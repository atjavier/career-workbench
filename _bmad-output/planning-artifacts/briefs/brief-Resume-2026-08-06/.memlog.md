- (event) Create intent confirmed; initialized a draft product brief workspace and loaded candidate evidence from the resume and four documented projects.
- (decision) The product is a private personal project. It will start with a user-selectable country preference, with the Philippines as the initial target market.
- (decision) Beyond discovery and factual tailored-resume generation, the product will open application links, maintain an application tracker spreadsheet with necessary job and application data, and refresh job information periodically.
- (decision) User selected the coaching path for product-brief discovery.
- (decision) The MVP refresh model is manual only; no scheduled or background job refreshes, to avoid uncontrolled resource use.
- (decision) The MVP will support company careers pages and permitted feeds plus user-configured search URLs for JobStreet, Kalibrr, LinkedIn, and similar platforms, subject to each platform's allowed access methods.
- (decision) The application tracker should integrate with Google Sheets, requiring user-authorized Google access and explicit handling of job-search data privacy.
- (decision) For a selected role, the tool should generate both a factually grounded tailored resume and a matching cover-letter draft.
- (decision) Default eligible roles are fresh-graduate-appropriate software engineering opportunities, including junior and associate roles, cadetships, paid training programs, and comparable entry pathways.
- (decision) Work-style preference is remote first, then hybrid, then onsite. Hybrid or onsite roles should preferentially be in the National Capital Region (NCR).
- (decision) Jobs with experience requirements beyond the candidate profile must remain visible but be labeled by acceptance likelihood, from most likely through stretch opportunities, rather than excluded.
- (decision) User approves a one-time Google OAuth sign-in so the private tool can create and update an application-tracking spreadsheet in the user's own Google Drive.
- (decision) Every tailored resume and cover letter requires user review and optional editing before export; the immutable base resume remains unchanged.
- (decision) The tracker uses Saved, Reviewing, Tailoring, Ready to Apply, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Rejected, and Withdrawn stages. The Interviewing stage must also support multiple interview rounds or other intermediate recruiting steps.
- (decision) User approves sending the minimum necessary resume facts and job-posting content to an external AI service for tailored materials, subject to clear provenance and privacy safeguards.
- (decision) MVP success is defined primarily by faster discovery of better-fit jobs and stronger tailored application materials.
- (assumption) The original requested acceptance-likelihood labels will be expressed as evidence-based fit levels, because the product cannot truthfully predict an employer's hiring decision.
- (decision) Tailored resumes and cover letters must be ATS-friendly: use conventional readable structure, include only truthful and evidenced role-relevant terminology, avoid keyword stuffing or hidden text, and preserve user review before export.
- (decision) Opportunity labels are Strong fit, Potential fit, and Stretch, accompanied by explicit evidence, gaps, and requirement conflicts rather than predictions of employer acceptance.
- (event) Completed the draft brief, incorporated the configured structural review by front-loading governing principles and consolidating handoff risks/open decisions, and confirmed no substantive prose change beyond correct Unicode rendering was needed.
